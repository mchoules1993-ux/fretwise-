# Sound kit and unlimited loop — plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-sound-kit-design.md`. Tests first for every task.

1. **Kit** — `site/sound-kit.js` (loaded after `sampled-audio.js`): `SOUND_KIT`, `RHYTHM_SOUND_IDS`,
   `CLICK_SOUND_IDS`, `kitInstruments(ids) -> ('guitar'|'drums')[]`,
   `playKitSound(ctx,id,when,{accent='medium',duration=.2,track=true})`. Add the file to
   `index.html` and to every test loader. Tests: every id plays on a fake context without errors;
   synth sounds need no samples; the strong accent is louder and higher than weak; drums/guitar call
   `sampledDrum` / `sampleVoice`; `off` plays nothing.
2. **Pickers and saving** — `rhythmOptions.rhythmSound/clickSound` replace `sound`; ids `rhythm-voice`,
   `rhythm-click`; saved under `fretwise.rhythm.v1`. `startRhythm` asks `getAudio` only for the
   instruments the chosen sounds need. Tests: pickers render and store; saved and blocked storage;
   instruments; count-in uses beep when the click is off; practice keeps the rhythm silent;
   demos still use the guitar.
3. **Unlimited loop** — loops `0` = “Until I stop”; bars scheduled ahead by a timer pump.
   Tests: finite runs still end after their bars; unlimited keeps scheduling bar after bar as time passes;
   Stop ends it, and in tap practice shows the score; stale timers do nothing after Stop.
4. **Checks and docs** — browser check (pickers, unlimited loop runs past 8 bars at a fast tempo, Stop),
   all required tests, catalogue, README/AGENTS, handoff entry.
