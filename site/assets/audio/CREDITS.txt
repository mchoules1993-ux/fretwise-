Fretwise audio sources — Release 8, 14 October 2026

Current instrument samples, all released under CC0 1.0 Universal:

Shinyguitar — Karoryfer Samples, recorded/mapped by D. Smolken.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.shinyguitar
82 electric-pickup samples: soft/hard attacks, two alternate takes per pitch,
release recordings, and two muted-string noises. Sixteen sampled pitches.

Black And Blue Basses — Karoryfer Samples.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses
36 fingered Darkblack bass samples: nine pitches, two dynamic layers and two takes.
Pitch labels adjusted for the library's transposing bass convention.

Virtuosity Drums — Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
32 acoustic kit samples: kick, snare, closed/half-open hi-hat, ride, crash and two toms.
Overhead microphone recordings; two dynamic ranges and alternate recordings.

Publisher confirmation of CC0 for the free libraries:
https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-samples
CC0 legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Copies of upstream licenses are in assets/audio/studio/*-LICENSE.txt.
File-level source paths and SHA-256 hashes: assets/audio/studio/provenance.json.

Preparation: selected recordings, bounded duration, mono downmix, 32 kHz sample
rate, common instrument gain and end fades, MP3 compression for web delivery.
Playback: dynamic-layer/alternate-take selection, pitch resampling, amp EQ,
crunch/lead shaping, envelopes, optional release noises, delay and compression.
The muted-string noise recordings are included for future articulation work;
current pitched muted notes use shortened sustained recordings.

Backing arrangements: composed and rendered for Fretwise using the above samples.
After midnight — slow minor blues in A, 72 BPM.
Dusty road shuffle — 12-bar A blues, 86 BPM.
Open road — 12-bar E blues rock, 104 BPM.
Pocket conversation — A Dorian funk vamp, 100 BPM.
Each has guitar, bass and drum stems plus a full mix. No artist recordings used.

artifact and is not requested by current playback.
